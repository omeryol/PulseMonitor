## English

- Fixed: after “Show desktop” (Win+D) the taskbar window could disappear until you clicked the taskbar. Hide and send-to-back requests from the shell are now undone immediately.
- Fixed: after “Show desktop” the weather card and the right-click menu opened in the top-left corner of the screen; they now open in the right place.
